A five-minute walkthrough of the project: the problem, how the drainage basins and covariates are built, what the models do, and where they succeed and fail.

**The numbers quoted in the video are out of date**, as explained in the [`Results & metrics`](kpis.md) section. Here are our updated claims:
- The models now have reasonably good `between-site R²` scores, and hence are capable of ranking unseen basins relative to one another.
- The regression model explains 43% of daily nitrate variation, approaching a good signal.
- The classification model catches 90% of violation days at a 54% false alarm rate.

Everything else in the presentation — the motivation, the data pipeline, and the reasoning behind the feature design — still describes the project as built.
